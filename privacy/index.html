Denying a permission may limit the corresponding feature.

For information stored only on your device, use the app’s available controls or the system’s app-data management options to manage it. We retain information you send directly to us, such as support correspondence, only for as long as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations. We take reasonable measures to protect information provided directly to us from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No transmission or storage method is completely secure. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you have supplied to us, or to exercise other rights available under applicable law.
